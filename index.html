Who am I?

I am a game devsigner(designer + developer) from Indianapolis, Indiana.

Recently, I graduated from Spelman College in Atlanta, Georgia with my B.S. in Computer Science with an Art Minor.

Where am I?

Currently based in Brooklyn, New York, I am obtaining my MFA in Game Design at NYU TISCH.

I have interned at companies like Microsoft, Verizon, and Electronic Arts (EA). Innovation Labs are my safe space.

What am I?

I am a multi-hyphenated artist, Mandarin Chinese student, and Calculus II enthusiast.

I create games to reflect my personal experiences through liberating marginalized voices via a queer Afrofuturistic lens.
